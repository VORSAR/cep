# S1.1c-2 plan check (v1): product review

Reviewer: product-manager. Read blind and read-only, against `s1-1c-2/integration` @ 50cf0f9. These are recommendations only; the owner decides (ground rules 1–2). One Hindsight search was run; it returned only generic pages and none of it is used here.

## Verdict (5 lines)

1. All nine items earn their place under the owner's ranking rule (decisions.md:624). The fragment test, the sleep fence and the anti-weakening pin protect the pattern engine most; the canary and `docsync` (a) protect it least.
2. The research is the plan's best part: it found silent passes (an empty vulnerability database exits 0, `-json` exits 0, a `replace` hides a module) and a red-on-main finding before any build.
3. One blocker: four SEMANTICS corrections are content changes to a document the owner decides, and the plan has the coordinator make them unasked, hidden among about 140 invisible markers under one "SEMANTICS changed" flag.
4. The size will almost certainly go past the cap: every recent sprint grew about 25–30% between card and build, the fragment item grew from 0.5 to 2.25, and there is 0.5 points of headroom. Apply the card's split line at G0, not mid-sprint; the card's line is the right one, because its first half guards S1.1c-3's refactor wave.
5. The owner's day-to-day experience needs four fixes: an expiry date should not turn a lane red on its own; a known issue's forward should expire; red scheduled runs need a short runbook; the summary should stay quiet when nothing changed. Several of the 14 questions need plain-language rewrites, and six questions are missing.
